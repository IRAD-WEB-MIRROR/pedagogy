History of Irad, grade 7: thematic plan 2026–2027

2 hours a week, 62 hours a year. Approved by the Academic Council of the Institute, minute No. 7 of 15 October 2026.

QuarterTopics
1st quarter (16 h)Irad in the Russian Empire: Syr-Darya Oblast, the settlers, the 1916 revolt; the 1917 National Council
2nd quarter (14 h)The Iradi SSR, 1925–1941: korenizatsiia, the Latin script of 1928, the founding of Kobozev; the repressions of 1937–38
3rd quarter (18 h)The war years 1941–45; the Iradi SSR after the war: the interceptor base, the Virgin Lands, Iradi State University (1958)
4th quarter (14 h)1989–2009: the end of the Soviet Union, 1992, the territorial period, the 1997 Language and Education Reform Act; revision

Changes from 2025–2026: the period 1992–2009 moves from grade 8 to the 4th quarter of grade 7 (2023 change to the standard). Grade 8 begins with the 2009 corridor protocol.

Textbook: History of Irad, grade 7 (2019). Teachers are asked to use the map of the ISEZ of 2026.
