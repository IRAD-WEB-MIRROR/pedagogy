Welcome

The Research Institute of Pedagogy prepares the state curriculum standards, the curricula and thematic plans for the public schools of the ISEZ, reviews textbooks and trains methodists of the district education offices. It publishes the journal Bilim jana Tarbiya (Education and Upbringing).
